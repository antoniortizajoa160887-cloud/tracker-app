Payroll Summary

Net pay = base pay + route pay (drivers) + additional income − active weekly deductions (claims + charges currently being deducted). Base pay is the flat weekly salary for Weekly people, or this week's Daily Pay total for Daily-rate people. Route pay, additional income, and daily pay all use the current week.
